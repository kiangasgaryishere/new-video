# دور دنیا با تن‌تن

Narration and Persian script supplied by the project owner. Country-name transliterations follow the supplied narration script. The illustrated itinerary highlights selected stops rather than depicting an exhaustive chronology.

The Tintin-inspired travel cut-outs and destination plates were generated for this reel. They are illustrative recreations, including the fictional countries. They are not archival scans or original Hergé panels. Character and book names refer to The Adventures of Tintin.

Cartographic outlines: Natural Earth, public-domain 1:110m land polygons, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ and https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson. The spinning globe uses the same modern land outlines. The reconstructed travel plates and imaginary destinations are illustrations.

Visual direction references Lemo Opuscar's Vintage Engraving Collage style, revision f9e204293930952b61ba921cd8944d05f121beee. All layouts and animation were made for this film.

Sound effects: Wyzowl SFX Kit, CC0 1.0, https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit. The cue sheet in sound.json records each sample, placement and gain. The additional wave, wind and steam sound bridges are synthesised in mix.py. No music bed.
